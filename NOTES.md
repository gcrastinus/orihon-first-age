# Notes

## Layout
Vertical gallery by decade (not a narrow accordion fold). Large cards; empty medium tracks are hidden.

## Scope
Italy and the Low Countries, 1250–1510 (extension: artists born ≤1454; see NOTES-extension.md). France and Spain excluded. A sparse “Ancient models” row holds monuments this generation looked at (Pisa Phaedra sarcophagus, Marcus Aurelius at the Lateran, Arch of Constantine) — not modern excavations like Pompeii.

## Vasari
Public-domain excerpts from Gaston du C. de Vere, Vol. I (Project Gutenberg #25326). Duccio, Coppo, Guido, Torriti, and the Magdalen Master have no dedicated Life (or only a note). Simone has a Life.

## Skipped or deferred
- Frederick II Capua gate head: no color ≥800×800 Commons file found.
- Maastricht Hours and other Low Countries illuminations: Commons folios were below 800×800 or rate-limited.
- Cologne choir / Utrecht Dom / Brussels Gudula: first Commons hits were wrong subjects (book cover, engraving, modern statue) and were discarded.
- Ypres Cloth Hall: post-WWI reconstruction — omitted rather than mislead.
- Santa Croce neo-Gothic façade omitted; Brunelleschi’s dome now included in the extension.
- Cimabue Assisi Crucifixion is shown in its oxidized “negative” state.

## Images
All JPEGs verified color with both dimensions ≥800. Data is inlined in `app.js` so double-click / `file://` works without a server.
