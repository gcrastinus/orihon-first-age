## Skipped (and why)
- **Michelangelo** (b.1475), **Raphael** (b.1483), **Titian** (b.~1488–90): born ≥1455.
- Extra northern painters (Rogier, Memling, Hugo): kept sparse — Campin + Van Eyck only.
- Luca della Robbia, Jacopo della Quercia, Michelozzo, Alberti: landmarks covered via Ghiberti/Brunelleschi/Donatello.
- **Filippino Lippi** (b.~1457): over cutoff.
- Buffalmacco kept because Vasari Life + Camposanto landmark; Bernardo Daddi for Giotto-workshop density.
